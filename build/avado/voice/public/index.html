Details

Everything stays on this box

  • Listening: Whisper turns your words into text on this AVADO.
  • Answering: Home Assistant handles simple commands ("turn on the kitchen light") itself. Anything else goes to your Private Assistant's model, which may also switch the devices you share with Assist.
  • Speaking: Piper reads the answer out loud, on this AVADO.
  • Memory: speech uses about 0.7 GB. Your Ethereum node still comes first: the Private Assistant pauses itself when the node needs the memory.
  • What changes in Home Assistant: a voice assistant called "AVADO voice" becomes the one Home Assistant uses. Switching off removes it and puts back the one you had.
